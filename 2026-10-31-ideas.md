# ElevenLabs project ideas (Hack or Treat, 2026-10-31)

Target category: **Best Project Built With ElevenLabs** (free tokens provided). This project focuses on this one category only.

What wins: **sound or voice is the product**. Use more than plain text to speech: sound effects, speech to text, voice changer, conversational agents, and cloning (only with the speaker's consent).

Each idea gives: pitch, how it works, why it wins, MVP then stretch, main risk.

## E1. AUTO-FOLEY: silent video in, full sound design out (suggested pick)
- **Pitch:** drop in a silent clip. A vision model writes a timed list of every sound the scene needs (footsteps on gravel at 3.2 s, a door creak at 5.0 s). ElevenLabs generates each sound effect, and the tool mixes a finished, synced soundtrack.
- **Why it wins:** the generated sound effects are the whole product, with a dramatic before-and-after demo.
- **MVP then stretch:** MVP is one clip in, mixed video out. Stretch goals are background ambience and an editable cue list with regeneration.
- **Risk:** timing accuracy, and the number of tokens used per clip. Cache sounds that repeat.

## E2. DEAD AIR: a live horror radio show the audience steers
- **Pitch:** a never-ending horror radio drama, generated and performed live with a full voice cast, sound effects and ambience. Listeners call in by voice ("don't open the door!") and the story changes on air.
- **Why it wins:** it uses many ElevenLabs features at once (many voices, sound effects, speech to text) in a format built entirely on audio.
- **MVP then stretch:** MVP is a 5-minute episode with 3 voices and sound effects, steered by typed input. Stretch goals are voice call-ins and a continuous stream.
- **Risk:** generation speed. Produce the next scene while the current one plays.

## E3. BLIND HOUSE: a haunted house you can only hear
- **Pitch:** an audio-only game played with headphones and your voice, with no screen. Footsteps behind you, a whisper in your left ear, a door creaking ahead. You move by speaking ("go left", "hide"), and sounds are positioned in 3D around you with browser spatial audio.
- **Why it wins:** a new kind of experience built only from sound. It's also playable by blind players by design.
- **MVP then stretch:** MVP is 3 rooms, positioned sound, and voice commands. Stretch goals are a character that talks back through an ElevenLabs agent and a scare system that reacts to how fast you answer.
- **Risk:** delay in recognizing voice commands. Keep a keyboard fallback.

## E4. MONSTER MIC: a live voice-changing costume
- **Pitch:** a wearable costume (mic, small speaker, phone) that changes your voice into a monster in real time using the ElevenLabs voice changer. Gestures or buttons trigger generated sound effects such as growls, chains or thunder.
- **Why it wins:** it takes ElevenLabs into the physical world. A perfect Halloween-night demo.
- **MVP then stretch:** MVP is a phone app that records, transforms and plays back with a short delay. Stretch goals are a near-live streaming mode and a sound board.
- **Risk:** check the voice changer's real latency early. If it isn't live enough, make it a "say it, then the monster repeats it" style.

## E5. FLUENT SELF: hear yourself speaking a language perfectly
- **Pitch:** language learners never hear what they would sound like when fluent. You record a consented sample of your own voice. ElevenLabs then speaks target-language sentences in **your** voice with native pronunciation. You shadow it (repeat right after) and speech to text scores how close you got.
- **Why it wins:** a new learning loop that only voice cloning makes possible. The speaker is also the person consenting.
- **MVP then stretch:** MVP is one language, 20 phrases, and a record-compare-score loop. Stretch goals are a progress chart and a conversational practice partner.
- **Risk:** cloning ethics. Only the user's own voice, with an explicit consent step and an easy delete.

## Practical notes
- The API key goes in `.env`, which must be listed in `.gitignore`. Never commit it, never send it to the browser.
- Free tokens run out. Cache generated audio during development instead of regenerating the same clip.
- Check current endpoint names, limits and pricing in the official docs (elevenlabs.io/docs) before relying on them.
